Group 8 · Serious Games Seminar

Diagnostic Framework

A structured evaluation tool for determining whether a digital game constitutes a genuine serious game — or surface-level gamification. Evaluate across five weighted criteria and five contextual conditions to produce a scored diagnostic report.

01 Design Logic Are learning objectives inseparable from gameplay?
02 Cognitive Demand Does the game require genuine systems thinking?
03 Failure Function Is failure educationally productive or merely punishing?
04 Learning Depth Is knowledge embedded — can you succeed without understanding?
05 Transfer Evidence Is there credible evidence of real-world skill transfer?
